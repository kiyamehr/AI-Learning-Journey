## 📘 About the Specialization

The Deep Learning Specialization, offered by **DeepLearning.AI**, covers the foundations of deep learning and neural networks, with a focus on both the theory behind deep learning and its practical implementation.

The specialization covers topics including:

* Neural networks and deep learning
* Hyperparameter tuning and regularization
* Convolutional neural networks
* Computer vision
* Sequence models
* Recurrent neural networks
* Natural language processing
* Attention mechanisms and sequence-to-sequence models
